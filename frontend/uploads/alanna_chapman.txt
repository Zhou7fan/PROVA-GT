About company: Most people think slavery was left in the previous century, but this couldn't be further from the truth. There are more people in slavery today than ever before. About interviewee: It was a Christmas Eve in Kolkata, India, when slavery become real to Alanna. She was visiting friends who run Freeset, a business offering alternative employment to women in prostitution. Deciding to visit one of the women she had met, she took a shortcut through an alleyway. In that moment, the penny dropped. She had passed modern day slaves. The injustice of it hit Alanna hard, until she had a 'what if" moment. What if she created a wine, where 100% of the profits go towards helping end slavery?
